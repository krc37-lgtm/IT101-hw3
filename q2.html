Website Usability Analysis

For analysis, i evaluated Blinkee.com which stands as a rather chaotic layout with poor interface design

10 Reasons why this Website is a bad design

  1. Chaotic Visual heirarchy: The page throws dozens of flashing neon items at the user simultaniously with no clear focal point.
  2. Poor Color Contrast: Bright neon text overlay complex backgrounds making it straining to read
  3. Visual clutter: Elements distract heavily from what the site can offer, working against it shopping function
  4. Confusing navigation: Product catagories as well as other functionality are compacted together, adding to the clutter
  5. Lack of Mobile Optimization:Desktop layout does not fluidly scale down to smartphone viewports, worsening the compact, cluttered structure of the site
  6. Too Much Going on: theres too many things to click and therefore makes it difficult to read the site
  7. Overwhelming Walls of Text: Massive chunks of text trying to get its point across while only adding to the issue
  8. Outdated Aesthethic: Appears to look like something from the early 2000's, decreasing customer trust with potential buyers
  9. Intrusive Pop-ups: Multiple overlapping elements that ruin readability of the site
  10. Autoplay Elements: Moving elements detracts only adding to the issue of lack of focal point and clutter
